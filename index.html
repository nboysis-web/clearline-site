The problem // 01

Your pipeline is full of
invisible holes.

Most consulting firms grew by doing great work -- not by building a revenue system. The result is a mess of spreadsheets, gut-feel forecasting, a CRM nobody trusts, and a sales process that lives in a founder's head.

01
Deals die in silence

No visibility into why proposals go cold. No follow-up sequence. No data on where qualified buyers drop out. Just vibes and hope.

02
CRM is a glorified contact list

Adoption is low. Data is stale. Your team logs calls inconsistently, if at all. Leadership can't forecast revenue with any confidence.

03
Marketing and sales aren't speaking

Leads come in and fall into a void. No handoff process, no qualification framework, no feedback loop. Revenue is siloed by accident.

04
You're flying blind on metrics

Win rate, sales cycle length, average deal size -- you have a rough guess, not a number. You can't optimize what you can't measure.
